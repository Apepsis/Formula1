Open methodology

A forecast you can audit.

RaceTwin separates pace, reliability and race randomness. Every output is timestamped, reproducible and compared with a simple grid-position baseline.

01

Pace model

Gradient boosting combines normalized practice pace, qualifying gaps, recent form and circuit characteristics.

02

Reliability layer

A separate hazard estimate prevents mechanical failures from being confused with driver performance.

03

Race simulator

Starts, weather, strategy, Safety Cars and non-finishes are sampled across 10,000 possible races.

04

Calibration

Walk-forward backtests measure Brier score, log loss and top-three coverage without using future information.
